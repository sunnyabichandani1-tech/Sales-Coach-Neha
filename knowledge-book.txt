BAJAJ EMI CARD: BENEFITS, COST, ELIGIBILITY AND KYC

==================================================

Name

1. Card name: Airtel EMI Card.

2. Other names used for the same card: Airtel Bajaj Finserv Insta EMI Card, Zero cost EMI Card.

3. The card is issued by Bajaj Finance in partnership with Airtel.

4. It is a digital card.



Benefits

1. No annual fee. The card is free for life.

2. One time cost: Rs 530, GST included, paid once at activation.

3. Rs 530 comes back as cashback in the Airtel Thanks App. It can be used in one go on an Airtel bill payment or recharge through the Thanks App. So the card costs nothing in the end.

4. Rs 1,000 voucher in the Bajaj Finserv app after the first in store purchase. The first purchase must be done within 30 days.

5. Zero cost EMI.

6. Accepted at 1.5 lakh+ partner stores, online and offline, across 4,000+ cities.

7. The first purchase must be offline, at a Bajaj partner store, to activate the card. The customer can find stores with the store locator.

8. EMI tenure: 1 month to 60 months.

9. Use: smart TVs, appliances, electronics, phones, even travel.

10. Easy approval and zero paperwork.

11. Card limit: up to Rs 3 lakh. The limit depends on credit score, credit profile and repayment history.



Eligibility and KYC

1. Age: 21 to 65 years.

2. Regular source of income.

3. Credit score as per Bajaj Finance policy.

4. Bajaj checks the customer's CIBIL score. The customer is told this before activation.

5. KYC for the card is done with Bajaj Finance, using Aadhaar or DigiLocker.

6. Details needed: PAN and Aadhaar number. No physical documents.

7. The process is fully digital and can be completed in the store.



How to get the card

1. Share consent and details for the eligibility check.

2. Complete KYC.

3. Pay Rs 530 and activate the card.

==================================================

Key hook for Pre to Post

"Everyone uses some network, but Airtel Postpaid is India's most powerful network with Fast Lane Tech, a dedicated priority network for you and your family. We also have the real king of family plans. May I quickly explain in 2 minutes?"



Probing questions

Postpaid probing

1. How many SIMs does your family use today, and what do you pay in total each month?

2. Are the recharge dates different for each number?

3. Do you run out of data before the validity ends, or buy data top ups?

4. Does anyone in the family travel abroad or plan to?

5. Which OTT apps does your family use today?

EMI Card probing

1. Are you planning to buy any new electronics, fitness equipment, or planning travel in the next few months?

2. Do you plan to buy it in one payment, or would monthly EMI suit you better?

3. Do you already have a card or EMI facility for such purchases?
